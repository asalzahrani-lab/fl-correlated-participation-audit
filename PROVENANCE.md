# Provenance: every reported number → script → output file

Numbering follows the extended version v13 (numbering unchanged since v8); the journal version is TIFS v8. `src/verify_claims.py` re-derives the headline numbers from the files listed here, and `REVIEWER_CHECKS.md` maps the reviewers' artifact checks to them. See also VERSION.md (versions and numbering) and CLAIMS.md (every headline number → script → output → value). The TIFS submission (v3 onwards) renumbers: its Tables I, II, III, V, VI are full Tables I, II, III, VIII, XI; its Table IV is a compact summary of full Tables VI–VII; its Fig. 1 is full Fig. 1; its Propositions 1–6 are full Propositions 1–6 (full Proposition 7 is stated in prose) and its Theorems 1–3 are full Theorems 1, 3, 4. Its Supplementary Material (6 pages) holds full Tables XIII, V, IV, VII, XI (both δ), XII as Tables S1–S6 and the Gaussian-fit theorem (full Theorem 2) as Theorem S1; full Tables IX–X and Figs. 2–3 appear only in the full version. All scripts are in `src/` unless stated; all outputs are kept in `results/` (logs in `results/logs/`). Scripts named `rev_*` were added in the revision rounds (`rev_cohort_example.py`, `rev_peruser_exact.py`, `rev_guess2.py` in the second; `rev_peruser_exact2.py`, `rev_timer_seeds.py`, `rev_t1000_bracket.py` in the third; `rev_timer_structural.py` and example (4) of `rev_cohort_example.py` in the fourth; `rev_round5_checks.py` and `rev_round5_upper_check.py` in the fifth; `verify_claims.py` in the sixth; `rev_round7_audit_sens.py` and `rev_round7_trace_sens.py` in the seventh).

Note on a column name: in `fl_mlp2_results.csv`, `fl_mlp3_results.csv` and `fl_mlp_5k_results.csv` the column `eps_true_model1` holds the in-script reference of the training scripts (12.08, an earlier 2M-sample value). The paper's reference is the 10M-sample 11.869 (Markov) and the exact PLD value 2.866 (i.i.d.); `derived_numbers.py` uses these.
